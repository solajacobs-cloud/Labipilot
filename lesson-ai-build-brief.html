Lesson one

Turn an idea into a build brief.

Before asking AI to build, decide what success means. This lesson gives learners a simple structure for turning a rough idea into clear instructions.

Objective

Write the brief before the code.

A useful AI build prompt should explain the user, the problem, the first release, the pages, the data, and the checks that prove the work is complete.

Brief structure

  1. Who is the user?
  2. What problem does the app solve?
  3. What should the first version include?
  4. What pages or screens are needed?
  5. What data must be saved or displayed?
  6. How will the result be verified?

Practical task

Write a short build brief for a waitlist app. Include the homepage, contact form, admin view, and Vercel deployment check.
